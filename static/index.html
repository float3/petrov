Petrov Day

a ritual for two countries

On 26 September 1983, Stanislav Petrov's early warning system reported incoming American missiles. He judged it a false alarm and did not pass it up the chain of command. He was right.

Two groups, two countries. Each can launch one missile at the other. The target sees a warning and a countdown, and may retaliate before impact. Detection systems occasionally raise false alarms. There is no score and no winner. Nothing happening is the good outcome.
